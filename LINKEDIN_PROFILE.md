## About

I am a laboratory analyst with experience in shared research facilities, analytical instrumentation, cleanroom and microfabrication workflows, laboratory safety, quality systems, and scientific data analysis.

At Bilkent University UNAM, I supported internal and external researchers through sample preparation, instrument operation, user training, competency assessment, troubleshooting, maintenance and calibration follow-up, technical documentation, and laboratory safety activities. My cleanroom and microfabrication experience includes photolithography, spin coating, mask alignment, UV exposure, photoresist development, thermal evaporation, oxygen-plasma surface activation, microfluidic-device fabrication, bonding, and microscopy-based characterisation.

Earlier, I worked through Monadelphous Engineering Associates on Chevron Australia’s Gorgon LNG Project, where I gained international industrial laboratory experience in process and quality-control analysis, ISO/IEC 17025 practices, sample traceability, reference-standard use, permit-to-work systems, and HSE-controlled operations.

My academic background combines biology, laboratory technology, and postgraduate biostatistics. I contribute to research involving biosensors, microfluidics, polymers and hydrogels, microneedles, spectroscopy, chromatography, mass spectrometry, microscopy, and experimental-data evaluation.

I also develop independent Python-based scientific software prototypes for laboratory and cleanroom workflows. Current portfolio projects include MEMS photomask design, lithography process tracking, photolithography process-window analysis, SEM metrology, DLS and zeta-potential analysis, scientific data preparation, chemical storage planning, and mass-spectrometry workflow integration. These projects are developed with explicit technical boundaries, traceability, testing, documentation, and staged releases.
