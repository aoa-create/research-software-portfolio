# LinkedIn Portfolio Launch Post

I have published a structured overview of eight scientific software projects developed or adapted as part of my independent research-software portfolio.

The portfolio focuses on practical laboratory, cleanroom, microfabrication, analytical-instrumentation, safety, traceability, and scientific-data workflows:

1. MEMS Photomask Designer — parametric MEMS patterns, multi-layer management, advisory geometric DRC, GDSII export, and traceable handoff packages.
2. Mask Process Traveler — SQLite-based lithography traveler, sample, mask, resist, operator, timestamp, UUID, and audit-event traceability.
3. LithoProcess Window — photolithography dose/focus process-window import, validation, analysis, and reporting.
4. SEM Metrology Suite — traceable SEM image calibration and controlled metrology workflows.
5. DLS Zeta Analysis Suite — DLS cumulants analysis, hydrodynamic diameter/PDI estimation, and zeta-potential calculations.
6. General Data Tool — laboratory CSV/Excel import, cleaning, header repair, matching, comparison, statistics, and export.
7. Chemical Storage Planner — conservative chemical-inventory and storage-planning decision support with explicit safety boundaries.
8. MS-Pipeline — staged mass-spectrometry workflow integration based on open-format processing and properly attributed MS-DIAL source components.

The projects are documented with explicit scope and limitations. Most currently use synthetic or demonstration data and are not presented as institutionally deployed, formally validated, or approved production systems. The objective is to build technically transparent, testable, traceable, and maintainable tools that can be evaluated and improved in controlled research or laboratory settings.
