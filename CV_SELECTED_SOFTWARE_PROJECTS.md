# Research Software Portfolio

Independent scientific and cleanroom software prototypes developed as
self-directed portfolio work. The projects use synthetic demonstrations and do
not claim institutional deployment, formal validation, fabrication approval,
or process sign-off.

## Selected cleanroom prototypes

### MEMS Photomask Designer
Parametric rectangle arrays, serpentine structures and interdigitated
electrodes; real GDSII export; SVG preview; advisory preflight; traceable ZIP
handoff package with SHA-256 checksums.

### Cleanroom Process Planner
Structured process-flow planning, step ordering, materials and review records
for synthetic cleanroom workflows.

### Lithography Run Tracker
Synthetic lithography run records, exposure/development observations and
traceable comparison outputs.

### Cleanroom Equipment Qualification Manager
Equipment qualification, maintenance, calibration and evidence tracking for
synthetic examples.

### Cleanroom Safety Risk Assessor
Risk-register and control-selection workflow for synthetic cleanroom safety
scenarios.
